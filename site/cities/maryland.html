How does Maryland party bus rental work?

Maryland party bus rental combines a private vehicle, chauffeur and custom route into one reservation for the entire group. Party Bus R Us serves Montgomery, Prince George's, Howard, Anne Arundel and Frederick-area pickup points with 20-35 passenger buses. Every quote is based on the selected bus, date, total hours and exact route. The quoted total includes fuel, standard onboard amenities and 20% gratuity. Cross-border trips into Washington DC, Northern Virginia, West Virginia and regional destinations can be planned in the same reservation.

Maryland party bus FAQ

What Maryland cities do you serve?

Regular service includes Bethesda, Silver Spring, Rockville, Potomac, Gaithersburg, Germantown, National Harbor, College Park, Frederick, Annapolis, Bowie and Columbia. Additional routes are available by quote.

Can our bus go into DC or Virginia?

Yes. DMV routes often cross state and District boundaries. Include every address so the quote reflects total travel and driver time.

What is included in the price?

The quoted total includes the private bus, professional chauffeur, fuel, route planning, standard onboard amenities and 20% gratuity.

Can we book late at night?

Yes. Booking and dispatch are open 24/7 by phone, text, WhatsApp and online quote request.
