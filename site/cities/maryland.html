How does Maryland party bus rental work?

Maryland party bus rental combines a private vehicle, chauffeur and custom route into one reservation for the entire group. Party Bus R Us serves Montgomery, Prince George's, Howard, Anne Arundel and Frederick-area pickup points with 20-35 passenger buses. Every quote is based on the selected bus, date, total hours and exact route. Ask the written quote to identify included items, gratuity and any additional charges. Cross-border trips into Washington DC, Northern Virginia, West Virginia and regional destinations can be planned in the same reservation.
