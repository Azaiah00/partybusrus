Common questions

Before you book.

How do I check availability?+
Send your date, approximate times and passenger count through our quote form, or call (703) 399-4394. A quote request does not reserve a bus; confirm your reservation directly with our team.
Which size bus do we need?+
Our fleet pages list options from 20 to 35 passengers. Count everyone who will ride, including chaperones. Ask us to confirm approved seating and space for belongings on the assigned vehicle.
What will the trip cost?+
Your date, hours, route and vehicle affect the quote. Ask for the total and a breakdown of any gratuity, tolls, parking, overtime and other charges before paying.
What are the payment and cancellation terms?+
Your written quote and booking agreement specify the deposit, payment deadlines, cancellation/refund and weather terms. Review them before paying.
What should we confirm for the journey?+
Confirm safe pickup and drop-off points, seating and accessibility requirements, any food or drink rules, and who your group should contact on the day. Passengers should remain seated while the vehicle is moving.
