Common questions

Before you book.

How far in advance should we book?+
Weddings: 4–6 months. Prom: book by February for May–June. Bachelorette / nightlife: 2–4 weeks. NYE, Halloween, July 4: at least 60 days. Last-minute openings appear weekly — text us if your date is tight.
Is BYOB allowed?+
Yes in Virginia and Maryland — bring your own. By state law we can't sell or serve alcohol on the bus. In DC proper, service requires an ABRA permit. All passengers must be 21+ for alcohol on board.
What's included in the rate?+
Everything except your bar: vehicle, professional chauffeur, 18% gratuity, fuel, basic decor (lighting, sound), pre-route planning, and 15 minutes pre-arrival. You cover BYOB, food, custom decor, and tolls outside the DMV.
What's the cancellation policy?+
Full refund 30+ days before your event. 50% refund 14–30 days. Deposit forfeit inside 14 days. Weather or driver-cancelled bookings get full refunds or a free reschedule.
Are your drivers and buses licensed?+
All drivers hold commercial CDLs with passenger endorsement and undergo background checks & drug screening. Every vehicle is inspected weekly and deep-cleaned between every booking.
