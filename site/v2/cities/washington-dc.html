Washington DC party bus FAQ

How much does a party bus cost in Washington DC?

Pricing depends on the bus, day, event date, route and total hours. Rentals use a four-hour minimum. The quote includes the chauffeur, fuel, standard amenities and 20% gratuity.

Can you pick up outside DC and finish in the District?

Yes. Many routes begin in Northern Virginia or Maryland and continue into DC. Include every pickup and final drop address so travel time is priced correctly.

Can we make multiple stops?

Yes. Multi-stop plans are common for weddings, nightlife, tours and corporate events. Stops should be scheduled before the trip so the driver can confirm legal loading access.

How far in advance should we book?

Book weddings and prom dates several months ahead. For ordinary weekends, two to four weeks is a useful target, but the 24/7 team can check last-minute availability.
