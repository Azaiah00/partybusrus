## Evidence-based note

Google states that AI-search optimization uses the same core foundation as SEO: indexable content, strong page experience, useful original information, internal links, accurate structured data and a current Business Profile. `llms.txt`, AI-specific markup and artificial phrasing are not substitutes for those fundamentals.
